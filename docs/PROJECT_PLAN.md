# Project Plan

## Outcome

Deliver a living innovation radar that discovers, stores, explains, filters, and refreshes specific opportunities for Orange Business Belgium. Global evidence is accepted when its Belgian or European relevance is stated.

## Definition of a complete topic

Every opportunity is `Vertical x Use Case x Technology` and includes attractiveness, right to win, urgency, confidence, dated evidence, signal type, why hot now, why it matters, and a concrete next action.

## Workstreams

1. Collection: RSS registry, priority sources, custom URLs, deduplication, source health.
2. Intelligence: relevance extraction, taxonomy, evidence claims, opportunity grouping, narrative synthesis.
3. Decision model: attractiveness, separate right to win, confidence, urgency, watchlist and publication rules.
4. Product: visual radar, focused cards, multi-select filters, persona views, evidence details, history, refresh control.
5. Governance: source URLs, prompt versions, model/provider settings, score version, decision log, human review.

## Team allocation template

- D: source discovery, RSS reliability, article quality, ingestion improvements.
- A: scoring research, Orange strategy/right-to-win evidence, metric justification.
- I: interface mapping, role journeys, visual validation, usability and presentation.
- I.: integration, agent workflow, prompts/models, database, testing, presentation coherence.

Initials are used intentionally. Team members can replace or split modules without changing the full pipeline contract.

## Presentation flow

1. Show the vague input converted into an explicit decision process.
2. Refresh open-source signals and show persisted history.
3. Open one opportunity and trace every claim to evidence.
4. Explain AI proposes structured factors while Python calculates the score.
5. Change persona/geography/domain filters and show focused results.
6. Show a low-confidence topic staying on the watchlist with its missing next step.
7. Close with modularity: sources, providers, prompts, scoring, and UI can evolve independently.
