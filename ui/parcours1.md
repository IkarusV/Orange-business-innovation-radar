## Personas

### Strategist

Main question:
"Where should Orange Business pay attention?"

Needs:
- Market trends
- Attractiveness
- Urgency
- Strategic relevance
- Emerging opportunities

### Sales

Main question:
"Who should I talk to, and about what?"

Needs:
- Customer relevance
- Buying signals
- Vertical
- Account overlap
- Recommended sales action

### Presales

Main question:
"Can Orange actually deliver this?"

Needs:
- Technology
- Use case
- Orange capabilities
- References
- Partners
- Technical maturity
